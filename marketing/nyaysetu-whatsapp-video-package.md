# NyaySetu Marketing Video Package

## Video Goal

Create a polished 30-second vertical marketing video for Instagram Reels,
YouTube Shorts, WhatsApp Status, and Meta ads.

Core message:

```text
Legal help is now simple on WhatsApp.
Ask basic legal questions and book a lawyer consultation quickly.
```

## Video Topic

**Get Legal Help on WhatsApp in Minutes**

## Format

- Aspect ratio: 9:16 vertical
- Duration: 30 seconds
- Language: Hinglish
- Tone: trustworthy, simple, calm, modern
- Target audience: Indian users facing common legal issues
- CTA: Start on WhatsApp / Book consultation

## Music Direction

Use:

- Calm modern corporate beat.
- Soft piano or light synth.
- Tempo around 90-105 BPM.
- Avoid dramatic courtroom music.

Mood:

```text
Trustworthy, hopeful, simple, professional
```

## Editing Style

- Clean transitions.
- Fast first 5 seconds.
- Keep every text line short.
- Use WhatsApp-green accents.
- Use navy/white for legal trust.
- Keep footer disclaimer small but readable.
- Add subtle phone notification sounds.
