# V1 Legal-Drafting Decision Record

Status: **candidate decisions complete; licensed Maharashtra advocate
authentication pending**.

This record resolves the product and clause choices needed to prepare the
first candidate template. It is a conservative drafting recommendation based
on the official sources in the Source Register. It is not a representation
that an AI system is an advocate and it does not activate the product.

## Controlling approach

1. The product prepares one English Self-Service Draft for one completed
   residential premises in Maharashtra, one adult individual licensor and one
   adult individual licensee, for exactly 11 months.
2. The customer supplies and confirms every fact. NyaySetu does not verify
   identity, title, authority, encumbrances, property description or capacity.
3. The agreement states the parties' intention to create a personal residential
   licence, but never promises that a court or authority will accept a label,
   clause, remedy or outcome.
4. Registration, execution, identity checks, stamp-duty determination and
   government payment remain outside Draft Studio. The instructions state
   the licensor's section 55 responsibility and require both parties to
   cooperate.
5. No generated document carries an advocate name, signature, stamp,
   letterhead, certification or claim of matter-specific review.
6. Any answer outside the decisions below routes to consultation before
   preview or payment.

Maharashtra. Calendar-month arithmetic must handle month-end and leap-year
boundaries and must be shown to the customer for confirmation.

## Selected legal and operational terms

- **Permitted use:** private residential use by the licensee and named
  occupants only, subject to applicable law and lawful society/building rules.
- **No transfer:** no assignment, sub-licence, paying guest, commercial use or
  third-party possession.
- **Authority confirmation:** the licensor confirms authority to grant the
  licence. This is a customer statement, not a NyaySetu title opinion.
- **Possession:** on expiry or valid earlier termination, the licensee must
  peacefully return vacant possession and keys, subject to applicable law.
  The agreement expressly prohibits forcible dispossession, lockout,
  disconnection, seizure or other self-help.
- **Notices:** hand delivery with acknowledgement, registered/speed post or
  reputable courier to the stated notice addresses. Email or WhatsApp may be
  used as an additional courtesy copy but is not the sole contractual service
  method in V1.
- **Forum:** applicable Indian and Maharashtra law; competent court or
  statutory authority where the premises is situated, subject to mandatory
  law. V1 contains no arbitration clause and does not restrict statutory
  remedies.
- **Amendment:** only a written document signed by both parties and registered
  when law requires. Oral or chat changes do not alter the generated draft.
- **Execution presentation:** unsigned party signature blocks and two blank
  adult-witness blocks are provided. Customers must verify current IGR
  identity, biometric, witness, execution and registration requirements.
- **Stamp duty:** the draft shows a factual consideration summary but not a
  final payable amount. Users are directed to the current Maharashtra IGR
  process/calculator because rates, inputs and procedure can change.

## Customer-facing limitations

Before confirmation and again in both outputs, state in plain language:

- this is a Self-Service Draft prepared from customer-provided facts;
- identity, title, authority and facts have not been verified;
- this is not matter-specific legal advice or advocate approval;
- the parties must review every fact and term before signing;
- stamp duty, execution and compulsory registration are still required
  external steps; and
- an ineligible, disputed, unusual or uncertain matter should be reviewed by
  an independent advocate.
