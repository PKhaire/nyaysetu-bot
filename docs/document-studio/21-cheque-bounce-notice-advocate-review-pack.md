1. Supported individual loan, exact cheque amount and clear memo.
2. Goods invoice with partial documentary support.
3. Receipt-of-information date near the proposed notice-window boundary.
4. Cheque presented outside current validity guidance.
5. Amount mismatch by one digit and by formatting/rounding.
6. Part-payment before presentation and after dishonour.
7. Company drawer/director request.
8. Multiple cheques and successive presentation.
9. Prior notice already sent.
10. Drawer address uncertain, refused, unclaimed or returned.
11. Customer alleges fraud/cheating in free text.
12. Payment captured but advocate requests a factual correction.
13. Post-approval artifact mutation attempt.
14. Customer reports full payment before dispatch and after dispatch.
15. Cross-user/cross-advocate evidence-access attempt.
