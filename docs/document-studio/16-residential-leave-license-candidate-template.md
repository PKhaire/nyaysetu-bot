## Clauses

### 1. Customer statements and authority (`LL-MH-001`, `LL-MH-002`)

Each Party states that the Party is an adult, understands this Agreement and
acts for themself. The Licensor states that the Licensor has lawful authority
to permit the residential use described below and that, to the Licensor's
knowledge, there is no conflicting ownership, possession, tenancy, licence or
eviction dispute affecting that permission. These statements are made by the
Parties and have not been independently verified by NyaySetu.

### 2. Premises (`LL-MH-003`, `LL-MH-S01`)

The premises are the completed residential premises described in Schedule 1,
including only the specifically identified included areas (the **Premises**).
The Parties confirm that the description is sufficient to identify the
Premises and matches the records they will use for execution and registration.

### 3. Residential licence (`LL-MH-004`)

For the Term, the Licensor gives the Licensee a personal and non-transferable
permission to occupy the Premises only as a private residence with the named
occupants. The Parties do not intend this Agreement to transfer any ownership
or other interest in the Premises, create a lease or tenancy, or give the
Licensee a transferable right. The legal character and effect of the
arrangement remain subject to the applicable law and the actual facts.

### 4. Term and renewal (`LL-MH-005`)

The licence begins on `[commencement_date]` and ends on `[expiry_date]`, a fixed
term of 11 calendar months (the **Term**), unless validly terminated earlier
under this Agreement and applicable law. There is no lock-in period and no
automatic renewal. Continued occupation after the Term requires a new written
agreement signed by both Parties and registered when required by law.

### 5. Licence fee and payment (`LL-MH-006`)

The Licensee shall pay a monthly licence fee of INR
`[monthly_licence_fee_inr]` (`[monthly_licence_fee_words]`) on or before day
`[fee_due_day]` of each month by `[fee_payment_mode]`. The receiving Party shall
provide an appropriate receipt or payment acknowledgement. This Agreement
does not contain or require either Party's bank credentials.

### 6. Refundable security deposit (`LL-MH-007`)

The Licensee shall pay an interest-free refundable security deposit of INR
`[refundable_deposit_inr]` (`[refundable_deposit_words]`) (the **Deposit**).
The Deposit is not licence fee and is not automatically forfeited.

After the Licensee peacefully returns vacant possession and all keys, the
Licensor shall, within seven Business Days, give an itemised written statement
and refund the undisputed balance. Deductions are limited to unpaid amounts
expressly payable by the Licensee under this Agreement, metered utilities borne
by the Licensee, and documented reasonable repair or replacement cost for
damage beyond ordinary wear and tear caused by the Licensee, named occupants
or their guests.

If an actual metered-utility bill is not yet available, the Licensor may retain
only a reasonable itemised estimate and shall refund any remaining balance
within seven Business Days after receiving the actual bill. **Business Day**
means a day other than Saturday, Sunday or a public holiday in Maharashtra.

### 7. Charges, utilities and repairs (`LL-MH-008`, `LL-MH-013`)

The Licensor shall pay property taxes, ordinary society/building maintenance
charges and the cost of structural or major repairs not caused by the
Licensee, named occupants or their guests. The Licensee shall pay metered
electricity, gas, water and other consumption or personal-service charges for
the Licensee's use, perform ordinary housekeeping, promptly report material
defects, and bear documented damage caused by the Licensee, named occupants or
their guests beyond ordinary wear and tear. Nothing creates an uncapped,
automatic repainting or replacement charge.

### 8. Occupants and inventory (`LL-MH-009`, `LL-MH-010`, `LL-MH-S02`)

Only the Licensee and the persons named in Schedule 1 may reside in the
Premises. A named occupant is not made a party or given an independent right by
being named. If Schedule 2 is present, the Parties shall jointly check the
listed items and visible condition at handover. Schedule 2 is not a valuation
or proof of title.

### 9. Use and conduct (`LL-MH-011`)

The Licensee shall use the Premises only as a private residence, comply with
applicable law and lawful society/building rules, avoid nuisance or illegal
activity, and not make structural alteration without the Licensor's prior
written consent and any approval required by law.

### 10. No transfer or third-party possession (`LL-MH-012`)

The Licensee shall not assign or transfer this licence, grant a sub-licence,
create a paying-guest or co-living arrangement, use the Premises commercially,
or part with possession in favour of a person other than a named occupant.

### 11. Inspection and emergency access (`LL-MH-014`)

The Licensor may inspect the Premises at reasonable frequency and during
reasonable daytime hours after giving at least 24 hours' written notice and,
where reasonably possible, when the Licensee or a representative is present.
Prior notice is not required for a genuine emergency presenting an immediate
risk to persons or the Premises, but access must be limited to addressing that
emergency and the Licensee must be informed as soon as reasonably possible.

### 12. Termination (`LL-MH-015`)

Either Party may terminate this Agreement without cause by giving the other
Party at least 30 calendar days' written notice.

For a material breach capable of remedy, the non-defaulting Party shall first
give written notice describing the breach and allow seven calendar days after
receipt to remedy it. If it is not remedied within that period, the
non-defaulting Party may terminate by a further written notice, subject to
applicable law. A Party may seek urgent lawful relief for illegal use or an
immediate risk to persons or property. This clause does not authorise forcible
dispossession, lockout, entry, disconnection, seizure or any other self-help.

### 13. Return of possession (`LL-MH-016`)

On expiry of the Term or valid earlier termination, the Licensee shall
peacefully return vacant possession of the Premises and all keys, remove the
Licensee's belongings, and leave the Premises reasonably clean, subject to
ordinary wear and tear. Recovery of possession and every remedy remain subject
to applicable law and lawful process.

### 14. Notices (`LL-MH-017`)

A contractual notice shall be in writing and delivered by hand with
acknowledgement, registered or speed post, or reputable courier to the notice
address stated for the receiving Party. A Party shall promptly give written
notice of an address change. Email or WhatsApp may carry an additional courtesy
copy but is not, by itself, the V1 contractual service method.

### 15. Stamp duty, execution and registration (`LL-MH-018`)

The Parties acknowledge that agreements for leave and licence of premises in
Maharashtra are required to be in writing and registered under section 55 of
the Maharashtra Rent Control Act, 1999. The responsibility to have the
Agreement registered rests on the Licensor under that section, and both Parties
shall reasonably cooperate in correct stamping, execution and registration.

The consideration summary in Schedule 1 is derived from customer-provided
facts and is not a stamp-duty assessment. Before execution, the Parties shall
use the then-current Maharashtra Department of Registration and Stamps process
to determine and pay the correct stamp duty and registration charges and to
confirm identity, biometric, witness and document requirements.

### 16. Governing law and forum (`LL-MH-019`)

This Agreement is governed by the applicable laws of India and Maharashtra.
Subject to any mandatory statutory forum or remedy, a competent court or
authority for the place where the Premises is situated may hear a dispute.
Nothing in this Agreement excludes a non-waivable right or remedy.

### 17. Entire understanding and amendment (`LL-MH-020`)

This Agreement and its schedules record the Parties' complete understanding
about this licence. An amendment must be in writing, signed by both Parties and
registered when required by law. If a provision is invalid or unenforceable,
it shall be applied to the lawful extent possible and the remaining provisions
shall continue, subject to mandatory law. Delay in exercising a right is not,
by itself, a waiver.
